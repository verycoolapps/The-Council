# 🇬🇧 English

## What is THE-COUNCIL?

Most AI tools give you one perspective. One opinion. One angle.

**THE-COUNCIL gives you a war room.**

When you face a complex decision — a business bet, an investment, a technical architecture, a strategic pivot, a risky idea — THE-COUNCIL assembles a board of specialized analytical personas. Each one independently examines your problem from a completely different professional lens. Then they argue. They challenge each other's assumptions. They find what you missed. They stress-test the strongest proposals. And finally — after intellectual battle — **one verdict emerges.**

This is not roleplay. This is not a novelty. This is **structured adversarial reasoning** — the same kind of intellectual rigor that elite strategy firms, military planning cells, and high-stakes investment committees have used for decades. Now packaged as a single Claude Code skill.

> *"THE-COUNCIL does not exist to make you feel right. It exists to make your decisions harder to break."*

<br>

## Why THE-COUNCIL Exists

Bad decisions don't fail because people are stupid. They fail because:

- Everyone agrees too quickly *(groupthink)*
- The strongest objection was never raised *(echo chamber)*
- A hidden assumption was never challenged *(blind spot)*
- The second-order consequence was never modeled *(short-term thinking)*
- The human element was never considered *(overconfidence in logic)*

THE-COUNCIL is engineered specifically to prevent all five.

## The Debate Engine — 7 Rounds of Intellectual Combat

```
ROUND 1 → OPENING POSITIONS        Each persona stakes an independent position
ROUND 2 → INTELLECTUAL COLLISION   Personas directly challenge each other
ROUND 3 → EVIDENCE WAR             Every major claim is stress-tested
ROUND 4 → DEVIL'S ADVOCATE ATTACK  Full adversarial assault on leading proposal
ROUND 5 → COUNTERATTACK & DEFENSE  Positions defended, modified, or withdrawn
ROUND 6 → FINAL POSITIONS          Each persona commits to a final stance
ROUND 7 → THE CHAIRMAN'S VERDICT   One synthesized, earned recommendation
```

## Advanced Analytical Tools

THE-COUNCIL applies these frameworks when relevant:

- **First-Principles Thinking** — Break to fundamental truths
- **Second-Order Thinking** — Model consequences beyond the immediate
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis** — Imagine failure and work backwards
- **Inversion Thinking** — What guarantees failure? Avoid that.
- **Opportunity Cost Analysis** — What is sacrificed by choosing this?
- **Scenario Planning** — Test across Best / Base / Adverse / Disruption cases
- **Sensitivity Analysis** — Which assumption, if wrong, breaks everything?
- **Regret Minimization** — Which decision survives 10 years of hindsight?
- **Bias Detection** — Confirmation, anchoring, survivorship, groupthink, sunk-cost
- **Contrarian Thinking** — What do smart dissenters actually believe?
- **Systems Thinking** — Map feedback loops and unintended consequences
- **Reversibility Assessment** — Is this decision undoable?

# 🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia

## Apa itu THE-COUNCIL?

Kebanyakan tool AI memberimu satu perspektif. Satu opini. Satu sudut pandang.

**THE-COUNCIL memberimu war room penuh.**

Ketika kamu menghadapi keputusan penting — taruhan bisnis, investasi, arsitektur teknis, pivot strategis, atau ide berisiko tinggi — THE-COUNCIL mengumpulkan dewan para spesialis analitis. Setiap anggota dewan memeriksa masalahmu dari sudut pandang profesional yang sepenuhnya berbeda. Kemudian mereka berdebat. Mereka menantang asumsi satu sama lain. Mereka menemukan apa yang kamu lewatkan. Mereka menekan uji proposal terkuat. Dan akhirnya — setelah pertempuran intelektual — **satu keputusan lahir.**

Ini bukan roleplay. Ini bukan gimmick. Ini adalah **penalaran adversarial terstruktur** — jenis ketajaman intelektual yang telah digunakan oleh firma strategi elite, sel perencanaan militer, dan komite investasi bertaruhan tinggi selama puluhan tahun. Kini dikemas sebagai satu skill Claude Code.

> *"THE-COUNCIL tidak hadir untuk membuatmu merasa benar. Ia hadir untuk membuat keputusanmu lebih sulit dipatahkan."*

<br>

## Kenapa THE-COUNCIL Ada?

Keputusan buruk tidak gagal karena orangnya bodoh. Mereka gagal karena:

- Semua orang terlalu cepat setuju *(groupthink)*
- Keberatan terkuat tidak pernah diangkat *(echo chamber)*
- Asumsi tersembunyi tidak pernah ditantang *(blind spot)*
- Konsekuensi kedua tidak pernah dimodelkan *(berpikir jangka pendek)*
- Faktor manusia tidak pernah dipertimbangkan *(terlalu percaya diri pada logika)*

THE-COUNCIL dirancang khusus untuk mencegah kelima hal tersebut.
